# Travel — Tokyo landing

A dark cinematic travel landing page built on one enormous photographic hero, with an
information strip cutting across its lower edge.

## What makes it work

The type scale is violently bimodal — a display heading at roughly ten times body size, and
then nothing until 11–12px. There is no mid-tier heading anywhere. That gap is what makes the
hero read as a poster rather than as a web page, and it costs nothing to reproduce.

The second move: the three-column teaser strip sits *inside* the hero's lower edge rather than
below it, so the photograph is cropped by content instead of ending at a section boundary.

## Layout

Full-bleed photographic hero, roughly 70vh, with a dark overlay graded so the top-left stays
darkest — display type is placed exactly where the image is quietest.

Nav is a thin overlay bar on the image, separated by a hairline rule rather than a background fill.
A vertical numbered rail (01–05) sits on the right edge at optical centre, active item enlarged and
white, inactive muted, with a horizontal rule extending right from the active number.

The lower hero band carries three equal columns of small body copy, each with an uppercase
call-to-action and a trailing arrow. Beneath them, a full-width hairline in which roughly one third
is accent-coloured — a progress indicator disguised as a divider.

The tour section is centred: a small muted caption *above* a larger uppercase heading — caption
first, heading second, which inverts the usual order and reads as a subtitle promoted to a kicker.
Four equal cards follow, each a photograph with a bottom gradient and a white uppercase label.

The closing section is a second full-bleed image — cosmic gradient, deep blue into magenta — with
display type left-aligned, a circular play control, a thin diagonal rule, and two video thumbnails
overlapping the lower boundary.

## Typography

Display: heavy grotesque, uppercase, tight leading (~0.88), no letter-spacing. Set very large.

Everything else: 11–12px, uppercase for labels and navigation with generous tracking, sentence case
for body. Weight contrast does almost no work here — size and case do all of it.

## Colour

Near-black cool base. Orange appears in exactly three places: the logo dot, the active nav underline,
and the progress hairline. It never fills a surface, never sits behind text, never appears in a card.
That restraint is why one warm hue reads as expensive rather than as a template.

Body copy is a muted blue-grey, never pure white — white is reserved for display type and card labels.

## Shape & depth

Effectively none. No radii, no shadows, no cards-with-borders. Separation is done entirely with
hairlines and photographic contrast. The tour cards use a thin offset frame rather than a fill.

## Motion

Implied only: the numbered rail and the accent progress hairline both indicate a slider.

## Anti-patterns it avoids

- No rounded cards, no card-within-card
- No gradient buttons — calls to action are bare uppercase text with an arrow
- No mid-tier heading sizes softening the display/body jump
- No accent colour used as a surface fill

## Reproducing this without the photography

The structure survives placeholders; the *impact* does not. At variant fidelity, judge the
bimodal type scale, the hero-cropping content strip, and the accent discipline. Expect it to feel
flat until real imagery lands — that flatness is the placeholder, not the layout.

Imagery required: one wide atmospheric hero, four square destination shots, one cosmic-gradient
wide shot, two video stills. All are well served by stock photography.
